Our Story

Founded in 2005 in Shenzhen — the world's electronics capital — Onemark started as a small workshop with a simple belief: great displays deserve great manufacturing.

Today, with 80+ skilled technicians and a 3,000+ m² ISO-certified facility, we ship to 20+ countries across five continents — but our commitment to quality and partnership remains exactly what it was on day one.
