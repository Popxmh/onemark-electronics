HOW CUSTOMIZATION WORKS

From reference image to project-ready kiosk.

Send what you already have — a reference image, technical sketch, installation photo or target configuration. We will review the hardware structure with the factory team before giving a tailored proposal.

Start with your project brief →
  1. 01

    Share your requirements

    Application, reference image, screen size, operating system, required modules, target market and estimated quantity.

  2. 02

    Confirm the hardware solution

    We check cabinet structure, peripherals, branding method, packaging and suitable configuration with the factory team.

  3. 03

    Custom sample evaluation

    Standard custom samples are scheduled in about 25 days; expedited sample arrangements can be discussed for urgent projects.

  4. 04

    Volume rollout

    After sample approval, we align production, export packing and shipment documents to your rollout plan.
