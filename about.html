About Onemark

Founded in 2005 in Shenzhen, China, Onemark is a manufacturer of commercial displays and self-service terminals, integrating R&D, production and marketing.

Our 3,000+ m² facility serves clients across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East and Southeast Asia with OEM/ODM/SKD services.

We are not just a manufacturer — we are your end-to-end display partner, from concept to delivery.
