Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

ONEMARK Electronics (Shenzhen Onemark Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through our website, inquiry forms, email, WhatsApp and related business communications.

Information we collect

Depending on your inquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, country or region, project requirements, estimated quantity, and any message, image or document you choose to send us.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, recommend suitable commercial display or self-service kiosk products, prepare quotations, discuss customization, arrange samples, provide support, and communicate with you about your project.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with our authorized employees, factory teams, logistics partners or service providers only when reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, fulfill your project or operate our business. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data retention and security

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, business records, dispute resolution or legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services

Our website may use services such as website hosting, analytics, email, WhatsApp and Meta lead forms. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. When you submit information through a Meta lead form, Meta may also process it under Meta's Data Policy.

Your requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you, request correction, or request deletion where applicable. To make a request, email minghe@onemark-electronics.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business customers and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our data practices or legal obligations change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
